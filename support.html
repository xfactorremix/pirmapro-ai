Frequently asked questions

What can I do with Pirma Pro AI?

Create, customize, manage, sign electronically, save, and share professional contracts, documents, and receipts on your phone.

Do I need an account?

Yes. You sign in with your name and email address so your documents and signatures are saved to your account.

Where are my documents stored?

Your documents and signatures are stored securely in the cloud with your account, so they're available whenever you sign in.

How do I share a document?

Open the document and use the share option, then choose an app such as Mail or Messages.

Are electronic signatures legally binding?

In many places, electronic signatures are recognized by law, for example under the Philippine Electronic Commerce Act (RA 8792) and the US ESIGN Act. Validity depends on your jurisdiction and the type of document, and some documents require wet signatures or notarization. Pirma Pro AI does not provide legal advice; if you're unsure, consult a lawyer.

Is Pirma Pro AI free?

It's free to download. An optional Premium subscription unlocks additional features.

How do I cancel or manage my subscription?

Purchases are handled by Apple and Google. On iPhone, open Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions. On Android, open the Google Play Store > Profile > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends to avoid renewal. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

Sign in with the same Apple or Google account used for the purchase and use the Restore Purchases option in the app.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store. For Apple, visit reportaproblem.apple.com. For Google Play, visit Google Play refund help.

How do I delete my account?

Email djxfactorremix@gmail.com from the email address on your account and ask for your account to be deleted. We will permanently delete your account, documents, and signatures.

Troubleshooting

  • App won't load or documents aren't syncing: check your internet connection, then close and reopen the app.
  • Signature not saving: update to the latest version of the app. If it keeps happening, email us.
  • Can't sign in: make sure you're using the email on your account, or email us for help.
